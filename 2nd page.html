Why do we require verification codes?

Verification codes are used to support a 2-Step verification process. 2-Step verification adds an extra layer of security to your account, reducing the chances of someone stealing your account and using it to access your personal information. To break into an account with 2-Step verification, an individual would not only have to know your username and password, they would also have to gain access to your mobile phone or email address.

Enter code

Enter the code that is sent to your preferred option (email or SMS). If you don't have your preferred device nearby you can request a code be generated via an alternate channel. To do so select 'send a code to a backup channel'.

Didn't get your code?

If you don't receive your verification code after a few minutes, remember to:

  • check your junk mail
  • check the email address you entered is correct
  • select "Resend code" when it's available
  • contact us for further support
