Accessing services

From this page you can login to services you have access to by selecting the relevant service icon.

Adding services

We need to link your account with services that are relevant to your role in industry. Each service has different linking requirements, and once linked will grant you access to one or more distinct services. You can add as many services as relate to your situation.

To add a service select it on this screen. You will be navigated to the service to undergo its tailored linking process if you have not already. Once complete, you can return to PRODA to add more services if you wish.

Managing services

Over time your situation may change, and you may need to amend your link to a service. To update your service specific information select the relevant service on this screen.

You will be navigated to the service to manage your information, and once complete will be returned to PRODA to continue your work.
